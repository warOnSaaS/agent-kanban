# Start here

Type **start** in a new chat. After that, these are all you need:

| Type | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **check tasks** | What's assigned to you, new things first |
| **review** | Walks you through what's waiting on your review |
| **new task** | Creates a task. Your AI asks what, and who it's for |
| **assign task** | Gives a task to someone, or unassigns it |
| **hand off task** | Passes something to someone with a note on what's done and what's next |
| **status** | Every task and where things stand: to do, in progress, waiting, in review, done |
| **start** | The welcome screen again |

Everything else, just say in your own words. Examples below.

## See what's going on

- "What's on my plate?"
- "Show me my open tasks, and anything new I haven't opened yet."
- "What's waiting on my review?"
- "What's open for Acme?"
- "What's up for grabs?" (tasks nobody owns yet)

## Create a task

- "Add a task for Acme: draft the remote work policy, due Friday."
- "Add a task for Acme to set up their payroll export, and leave it unassigned."

If you don't say who, it's yours. Say "leave it unassigned" and it goes to
**Up for grabs**, where anyone on that client can take it.

## Assign a task

- "Give the remote work policy task to Jordan."
- "Assign Payroll export to me."
- "Hand this to Jordan: I drafted the policy, next they need to format it and send it to the client."

A hand-off records what you did and what's next inside the task, so the other
person's AI picks it up with the whole story. They get an alert.

## Unassign a task

- "Unassign the Payroll export task."
- "Take me off the handbook task, put it back up for grabs."

## When something comes back to you

Nothing to refresh. The workspace is one shared copy, so the moment someone sends
a task back, it is first in your **check tasks** under "new, not opened yet",
marked **SENT BACK** with what they want next, and you get an alert.

## Review something

When someone hands you work to review, it shows under **Waiting on your review**.

- "Open the handbook task and walk me through what Jordan did."
- "Approve it and mark it done."
- "Send it back to Jordan: change the PTO section first."

## Meet to discuss

Anything can wait on a conversation. You choose who's in it and who sets it up.

- "Mark the overtime task as meet to discuss, with Jordan. I'll set it up."
- "Payroll export needs a meeting with Jordan and me. Jordan sets it up."


## Notes, ideas and alerts

- "Save these call notes to Acme and turn the action items into tasks."
- "Put an idea on the board: a 30-day HR audit package for law firms."
- "Tell Jordan the client approved the handbook."
- "Show my alerts."

## Private things

- "Save this note to Acme, private to me."
- Anything in `internal/` is only visible to the owner.
