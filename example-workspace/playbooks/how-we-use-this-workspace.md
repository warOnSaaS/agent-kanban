# How we use this workspace

1. **If it has an owner and a date, it's a task.** Otherwise it's a note.
2. **Talk, don't file.** Tell your AI what happened; let it pick the folder.
3. **Hand-offs go through tasks.** Reassign the task; the new owner gets an alert.
4. **Alerts are for things someone must see today.** Mark them urgent only if it can't wait; urgent alerts are also emailed.
5. **Ideas are cheap.** Post half-formed ones. Comment on other people's.
6. **Private is the exception.** Mark a note private only when it really is (compensation, a sensitive client issue).

## Good things to say to your AI

- "What's on my plate?" / "What's overdue across all clients?"
- "Prep me for my call with <client>."
- "Save these notes to <client> and turn the action items into tasks."
- "Give the handbook task to <name>, due Friday."
- "Tell <name> the client approved the draft."
- "What has the team posted on the ideas board this week?"
